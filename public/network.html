The temperature

Every relationship has a temperature. Maria reads it.

Personal warmth and business trust are independent. A friend who's also a client can be 75 personal and 50 business. A vendor you respect but don't socialize with might be only business.

Maria updates these silently as you talk to her. The labels — NEUTRAL, FRIENDLY, TRUSTED, INNER CIRCLE — change as the temperature moves. Override any of it manually whenever you want.
